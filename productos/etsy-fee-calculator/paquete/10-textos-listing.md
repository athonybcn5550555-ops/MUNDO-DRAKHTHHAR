## Descripción

```
Know exactly what you earn on every sale, and what to charge to earn what you want.

Buy once. Works offline. No account, no subscription.

WHAT IT DOES
• Calculates Etsy fees per product: listing, transaction, payment processing, regulatory operating fee, Offsite Ads (with the per-order cap) and Etsy Ads
• Shows profit and margin per sale, with a green / amber / red signal so under-priced products are obvious at a glance
• Price finder: enter the profit or margin you want and it tells you the price you need, and how many sales a month to hit your income goal
• Unlimited products, each with materials, hours of work, packaging, shipping, print-on-demand cost and overhead
• One tick hides shipping and packaging for digital products
• Exports your product list to CSV and prints to PDF

FEES YOU CAN TRUST
Fee profiles for the United States, Spain, France, Italy, Germany, the United Kingdom, Canada and Australia — with the date they were last verified shown on screen. Etsy changes its fees; every single figure is editable, so the tool never goes out of date on you. VAT on seller fees and currency conversion are included as options.

FIVE LANGUAGES
English, Spanish, French, German and Italian, switchable at any time. Amounts and dates follow each language's own format.

WHY NOT A SPREADSHEET
No Excel, no Google Sheets, no Apple Numbers. No formulas to break when you clear the example data. No "make a copy" link, no account to create, no macro warnings. One file that opens with a double click on Windows, Mac, Linux, tablet or phone — even with the internet switched off.

WHAT YOU GET
• Fee-Price-Calculator.html — the app, one file, 54 KB
• Start-Here.pdf — quick-start guide in all five languages
• LICENSE.txt — personal and commercial use on your own computers

REQUIREMENTS
Any modern web browser: Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox. No installation. No internet connection needed.

YOUR DATA
Everything you type stays inside your own browser. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Export a backup file whenever you like and restore it on another computer.

HOW DELIVERY WORKS
Instant download. Files appear on your Etsy purchases page right after payment.
Please download from a web browser — the Etsy mobile app cannot download files. This is the most common question and it takes ten seconds to solve.

REFUNDS
Digital files cannot be returned. If it does not work on a browser that meets the requirements, message me first: I will get it running or refund you.

SUPPORT
Message support and minor updates included for 30 days after purchase. I answer within 24 hours.
```
