Offsite Ads are charged on the full order (price + shipping + gift wrap), which is why they cut margins more than the headline percentage. Etsy Ads are pay-per-click: enter what you actually spend per resulting order (Shop Manager → Marketing → Etsy Ads → cost ÷ orders). VAT on fees applies to EU/UK sellers without a VAT ID.

2 · Your products

Add every product you sell. Enter what you charge and what it costs you; the app shows fees, profit and margin per sale. Green ≥ 25% margin, amber 10–25%, red under 10% or a loss.

Three steps and you're done

  1. Pick your country in section 1 and glance at the fee table. If Etsy has changed a fee since the date shown, edit the number.
  2. Add your products in section 2 with price, shipping and costs. Profit and margin update as you type.
  3. Use the price finder in section 3 to work backwards from the profit you want.

Your data is saved automatically in this browser. Click "Export backup" now and then to keep a copy. Nothing is sent anywhere: this file works with the internet switched off.
